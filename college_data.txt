COLLEGE HELP DESK INFORMATION

ADMISSIONS
Admission requires the following documents:
- Class 10 marksheet
- Class 12 marksheet
- Government ID proof
- Passport-size photographs
- Transfer Certificate
- Migration Certificate, if applicable

EXAMINATIONS
Students must complete examination registration before the deadline announced by the examination department.
Students should carry their valid college identity card during examinations.
Students must follow the examination rules and instructions issued by the examination department.
Late registration may be subject to college rules and approval.

FEES
For fee payment issues, students should contact the Accounts Department.
Students should keep their payment transaction details or receipt when reporting a payment issue.

ACADEMICS
For academic-related issues such as course registration, subjects, attendance, or academic rules, students should contact the Academic Department.

DEPARTMENTS
Accounts Department:
Handles fee payments, payment issues, receipts, and financial queries.

Examination Department:
Handles examination registration, examination schedules, examination rules, admit cards, and results.

Academic Department:
Handles course registration, subjects, attendance, and academic-related queries.

Admission Department:
Handles admission procedures and admission documents.

HOSTEL
For hostel-related queries, students should contact the Hostel Administration.

LIBRARY
For library-related issues, students should contact the Library Department.

IMPORTANT
The information available in this knowledge base should be treated as the source of truth.
If a requested piece of information is not available, the agent should clearly tell the student that the information could not be found rather than inventing an answer.
